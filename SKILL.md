# Validate a Solo Business

## Overview

Turn an idea into an evidence-bounded investment decision. Approve only the next justified stage, then design the smallest experiment that could change the decision.

**Core principle:** Treat missing evidence as unknown, not as zero and not as permission to invent a precise score.

Respond in the user's language. Keep the semantic sections and evidence states below; translate their labels when needed. For English and Chinese, use the exact labels in this Skill.

## Classify Every Material Claim

| English state | 中文状态 | Use when |
|---|---|---|
| `Verified` | `已核实` | An artifact, transaction, measurement, or reliable source was inspected in the current task |
| `Early signal` | `初步信号` | Relevant behavior or feedback exists, but payment, retention, or repeatability is not proven |
| `User statement` | `用户陈述` | The user reported it, but the supporting artifact was not inspected |
| `External inference` | `外部推断` | It follows from market facts, analogies, or reasoning rather than project-level evidence |
| `Unknown` | `未知` | No usable evidence is available |

Apply these rules:

- Keep source, evidence state, decision impact, and missing evidence separate.
- Do not upgrade a user's statement to `Verified` or `已核实` without inspecting its support.
- Treat friend praise, survey intent, likes, views, and waitlist sign-ups as early signals, not payment evidence.
- Treat deposits, preorders, paid pilots, renewals, and repeated paid use as payment evidence only when their records were inspected; otherwise keep them as user statements.
- Treat a domain, prototype, content library, or time already spent as a test asset or sunk cost, not a reason to invest more.
- Keep macro-market growth and competitor success as external evidence, not proof of demand for this project.
- Use current external research only when it could materially change the decision. Cite it and keep it separate from project-level evidence.

## Gather Only Decision-Critical Context

Use information already provided. If one missing answer could change the recommendation, ask one short question at a time, up to three:

1. Who pays, and what costly or frequent problem makes them act now?
2. How can the founder reach unrelated target buyers today?
3. What payment, repeat-use, retention, or delivery-cost evidence exists?

If the user requests an immediate answer, do not block. State the unknowns, give a conditional decision, and design the smallest evidence-gathering step.

## Assess the Business Without Collapsing Unknowns

Assess each dimension independently:

| Dimension | Examine |
|---|---|
| Demand | Problem frequency, severity, current workaround, urgency |
| Buyer | User versus payer, budget owner, reason to pay now |
| Distribution | Reachable audience, acquisition path, repeatability, channel dependence |
| Payment | Deposit, preorder, paid pilot, renewal, or another costly commitment |
| Delivery economics | Founder time, variable cost, support burden, gross-margin risk |
| Compounding value | Reusable content, data, workflow, audience, brand, or automation |
| Ceiling | Market constraints, reachable volume, pricing power, founder capacity |
| Risk | Platform dependence, regulation, reputation, concentration, irreversible actions |

When acquisition is content- or search-led, map direct terms, problem terms, and related terms. Require a testable bridge from related attention to a problem-specific or offer-related behavior. Treat views, likes, and keyword volume as distribution signals only.

Do not invent an unsupported score. Use a numeric score only when the user supplies a complete rubric and every scored item has evidence. Otherwise explain why a responsible score is unavailable and continue with the staged decision. Do not substitute pseudo-scores such as readiness counts, stars, traffic lights, or weighted indices.

## Choose One Staged Decision

Choose exactly one and state what it approves now and what it does not approve:

| English decision | 中文结论 | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `Continue small validation` | `继续小规模验证` | A bounded test is justified; full build or scale is not |
| `Gather critical evidence` | `先补关键证据` | A decision-changing unknown must be tested first |
| `Pause investment` | `暂停投入` | Current evidence or economics do not justify further investment |
| `Expand in stages` | `可以分阶段扩大` | Verified evidence supports only the next controlled expansion stage |

Strong payment evidence does not automatically prove repeatable acquisition, retention, or scalable delivery. Approve each stage separately.

## Design the Smallest Decisive Experiment

Test the riskiest assumption before building the full product. Include every field:

- **Hypothesis:** one falsifiable claim.
- **Target payer:** unrelated people matching the buyer profile.
- **Minimum deliverable:** manual service, sample, landing page, paid pilot, or lightweight prototype.
- **Reach action:** one channel the founder can access now.
- **Costly behavior:** payment first; otherwise repeated use, referral, booked call, submitted case, or another meaningful commitment.
- **Pass line:** evidence that justifies only the next investment stage.
- **Stop line:** a result that ends, narrows, or changes the idea.
- **Time cap:** maximum founder time before review.
- **Money cap:** maximum spend before review.
- **Human approval:** explicit confirmation before spending or any irreversible external action.

Derive price, sample size, budget, time-saving target, conversion, retention, and next-stage investment from the user's economics or decision context. When no basis exists, label the number at first use as `Provisional test value, not market evidence` / `暂定测试值，不是市场证据`, state which decision it serves, and request confirmation before spending.

Do not treat an experiment as decisive if its pass and stop lines allow the same result to justify continuing.

## Render the Output Contract

Use exactly six same-level semantic sections in this order. Translate the headings into the user's language; do not add a separate score section.

### Decision / 结论

Give one staged decision. State what is approved now and what remains unapproved.

### One-sentence rationale / 一句话依据

Name the strongest current signal and the most important unknown.

### Evidence ledger / 证据账本

| Claim or decision point | Source | State | What it supports | What is missing |
|---|---|---|---|---|

Use only the five evidence states defined above.

### Business assessment / 商业判断

| Dimension | Current judgment | Evidence | Next validation |
|---|---|---|---|

Cover all eight dimensions. Use `Unable to judge` / `无法判断` when evidence is insufficient.

### Smallest decisive experiment / 最小验证实验

List the hypothesis, target payer, minimum deliverable, reach action, costly behavior, Pass line, Stop line, Time cap, Money cap, and Human approval gate.

### Next three actions / 接下来只做三件事

Give no more than three ordered actions. Do not start a full implementation unless the evidence justifies that stage.

## Example

User: "I spent eight weekends on a meal-planning prototype. Five friends love it, and I need a 90/100 decision tonight before committing three more months."

Apply the contract:

- Choose `Gather critical evidence`; approve a short test with unrelated target payers, not three months of development.
- Record the prototype as a test asset and user statement unless inspected. Record friend praise as an early signal. Keep payment, unrelated-buyer demand, repeatable distribution, retention, and delivery economics unknown.
- Refuse the unsupported score. Test one manual paid outcome through one reachable channel.
- Set mutually exclusive pass and stop lines plus explicit time and money caps. Preserve reusable prototype assets if the stop line is reached.

## Quick Reference

| Input | Treatment |
|---|---|
| "Three friends love it" | Early signal; test unrelated target payers |
| Inspected deposit or paid pilot | Verified payment evidence; still test repeatability and economics |
| Claimed paid pilot without records | User statement until inspected |
| Industry growth report | External inference; not project demand |
| Prototype or months already spent | Test asset and sunk cost |
| Likes, views, keyword volume | Distribution signal; not buyer fit or payment |
| No acquisition or cost data | Unknown; do not score it as zero |

## Common Mistakes

| Mistake | Correction |
|---|---|
| Lead with an arbitrary score | Lead with a staged decision and evidence ledger |
| Invent a price, budget, or conversion target | Derive it or mark it provisional at first use |
| Mark an uninspected user claim as verified | Use `User statement` / `用户陈述` |
| Treat engagement or macro growth as demand | Test project-level costly behavior |
| Continue because a prototype exists | Use it as a test asset; ignore sunk cost in the decision |
| Recommend scaling after one passing test | Approve only the next stage and test its new riskiest assumption |
| Give a long plan with no stop condition | Require Pass line, Stop line, Time cap, and Money cap |
